Brighter
Beginnings.

A 10-week peer support group for parents and carers in the north east of Glasgow who care for children and young people with additional support needs (ASN).

Come together to give and receive emotional, social and practical support. Shared experiences. Understanding faces. A little time for you.

You’re welcome here

Who can join?

Parents and carers living in the north east of Glasgow, with children or young people in their care who have additional support needs.

How to join

Parents and carers can refer themselves directly or be referred through a multi-agency referral. Use our registration form to tell us about your family and the support you need.
